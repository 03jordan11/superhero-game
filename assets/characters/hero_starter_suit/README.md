# Starter suit

The player's current superhero costume is `hero_starter_suit_rigged.glb`: one textured mesh, 2,172 triangles and the existing 65-bone gameplay rig. It includes the mouth and glove cleanup and uses the game's existing animation libraries. The unmodified upload is the rebuild input at `source/hero_starter_suit_original.glb`; `source/hero_starter_suit.blend` is the refined mesh before skinning, and `source/hero_starter_suit_rigged.blend` is the editable gameplay version.

Used by `scenes/player.tscn` and displayed as `Costumes/StarterSuit/Model` in `scenes/hero_costumes.tscn`. Rigging fits the stance and arms to the existing joint locations. Head, chin and upper hood follow Head rigidly; the lower hood blends into the neck, the hoodie hem follows the torso, and the mitten fingers bend together with separate thumb weights. Four normalized influences per vertex and matching seam weights prevent tearing. The original UVs, lip colors and embedded textures are retained.
