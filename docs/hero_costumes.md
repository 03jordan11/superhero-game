# Hero costumes

Open `scenes/hero_costumes.tscn` and press **F6**. The saved scene displays two outfits side by side: **Underwear**, using the updated rigged Meshy player with the corrected chin and chest, and **Starter suit**, using the new textured upload. Both stand at the same display height. Right-drag to orbit, scroll to zoom, use the view dropdown for either outfit, and press R to reset.

Running the scene starts both outfits in **Idle**. The animation dropdown includes the current player's movement, jump, flight, combat, hit and death clips, plus **Rest pose**. Both outfits always use the same timestamp. Click **Pause / Play** or press **Space**, choose **0.1×–2×** playback speed, or drag the timeline to pause at a precise pose. **Restart** returns to the beginning while retaining the paused/playing state. Clips loop for inspection, including one-shot gameplay actions; this preview does not simulate gameplay transitions. Selecting another outfit view preserves the current pose and playback time.

Animation-preview changes: `scenes/hero_costumes.tscn`, `scripts/previews/hero_costumes.gd`, `tests/test_hero_costumes.gd` and this document. Godot validation passed for every available clip at three timestamps on both skeletons, resolved animation targets, pause/scrub/speed/restart controls, view switching and restoring the rest pose. Idle, sprint and hover previews were rendered in Godot and inspected visually.

The outfits are explicit, editable instances under `Costumes/Underwear/Model` and `Costumes/StarterSuit/Model`. The studio, camera, lighting, plinths and labels are preview content. The scene does not spawn gameplay controllers or change the selected player/civilian model. Both outfits now use the existing 65-bone rig: the starter suit has 2,172 triangles and the underwear model has 1,552. `scenes/player.tscn` selects the rigged starter suit; civilian NPCs retain the underwear model.

Both models now have flatter lips and a local vertex tint to reduce the pale lip highlights. The suit's two glove hands use the underwear model's mitten shape as their reference, retaining the thumbs and blending back into the existing cuffs. These are vertex edits with no added triangles. The underwear model's hand geometry is unchanged. Each runtime costume includes its lip correction.

Consistency revision files: both runtime GLBs and editable Blender sources; Meshy `tools/costume_shapes.py`, `tools/build_meshy_rig.py`, `tools/import_lip_colors.gd` and `.import` settings; starter-suit `tools/refine_costume.py`, retained original build input and `.import` settings; both audit reports and READMEs; `tests/test_hero_costumes.gd`. Both exported meshes retain all three original texture-image byte streams and have no degenerate triangles. The costume test verifies imported lip colors remain local to the mouth. Costume, player and civilian regressions passed after these changes; front/profile mouth and both-hand close-ups were rendered and inspected. Keep the underwear mesh as the hand-shape reference for future AI-generated outfits.

To add an outfit later, duplicate an outfit container under `Costumes`, replace its `Model` instance with the new asset, update its name, `display_name` metadata and label, and position it beside the others. The view dropdown discovers these containers automatically. Adjust `overview_distance` if the lineup becomes wider.

The suit rig uses the original gameplay armature/rest transforms, with fitted stance and arms, rigid face/upper hood and coherent mitten weights. Existing controls, player collision and animation libraries are unchanged. Rigging files: starter-suit `tools/rig_costume.py`, `tools/audit_rig.py`, `hero_starter_suit_rigged.glb` plus `.import`, editable rigged Blender source and `rig_audit.json`. Integration updates: `scenes/player.tscn`, this costume scene, the current-player animation preview label and the two costume/player regression checks.

The player costume was checked across 14 clips at four timestamps against the original rig. Costume/civilian, pickup binding, laser-eye and hostile-grab tests passed. Export audit confirms 2,172 triangles, normalized weights, consistent seam weights and unchanged embedded texture bytes. Actual Godot renders of idle, sprint, charged jump, flight and hold/grab were inspected; manual city traversal was not performed. Restart F5 to use the suit and check movement, flight and grabs in-game.
