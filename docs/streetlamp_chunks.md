# Streetlamp chunks and distant levels

The 1,483 streetlamps generated by `SuperCity/NightLights` are now divided into **93 cells on a 250 m grid**. Roads, the blimp, and the separate harbor/riverbank lamp systems were not changed in this pass.

Previously the fixtures used four city-wide MultiMeshes: all posts in one, plus three lens-color batches. Those already had few submissions, but their large bounds kept full-detail geometry eligible to render across the city. This change trades more spatial batches for much less distant geometry and finer culling. It does **not** claim fewer draw calls than the old four batches, or a measured FPS improvement.

Each cell uses one single-surface MultiMesh at a time, sharing one fixture mesh and one material across its instances. District colors use per-instance data, avoiding separate color batches within each cell. This is Godot instancing, rather than duplicating every fixture's vertices into a large ArrayMesh.

## Default distance levels

Distances are 3D camera distance to the **nearest point of each cell's actual fixture bounds**, not to its center or each individual pole. A 25 m margin prevents repeated switching at one threshold.

| Level | Switch while moving away | Return threshold | Triangles per fixture |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Original-detail fixture | Below 150 m | Below 125 m | 228 |
| Simple pole, arm, housing and lens | Beyond 150 m | Below 350 m from head-only level | 48 |
| Tiny lit lens only, no pole | Beyond 375 m | Below 750 m from hidden level | 12 |
| Completely hidden | Beyond 775 m | Returns below 750 m | 0 |

The simple fixture drops the base and cylindrical detail. Head-only geometry is omitted entirely in daylight; its night emission fades with the same clock value and retains its original size and color. No oversized billboard or fake ground-light plane is added. Shadow casting remains disabled, as before.

Original whole-city fixture batches are hidden while this controller is enabled. They remain under `NightLights/OriginalFixtures` for comparison. The nearby fixture shape, placement, orientation, metal shading, district color and emissive lens match the originals.

## Actual illumination

The existing bounded pool of **96 pavement spotlights and 24 frontage lights** remains. Mesh LOD does not add real lights or expand their budgets. Pavement selection now uses camera-to-emitter 3D distance, so flying high above the city no longer allocates lights solely because the camera is horizontally near the pavement. The existing energy fade and frontage logic remain.

These fixtures had no collision; no collision was introduced or removed. The controller joins the existing loading preparation group.

## Tuning and comparison

Select **`Main/SuperCity/NightLights/StreetlampChunks`**. It is an authored scene node, so its exported settings can be saved in the Inspector. Runtime cell children appear underneath it in the Remote tree.

- `Enabled`: false restores the original city-wide batches and hides all replacement meshes.
- `Force Lod`: Automatic, Full fixtures, Simple fixtures, Heads only, or Hidden. Heads only remains invisible in daylight.
- `Full Detail Distance M`, `Heads Only Distance M`, `Cutoff Distance M`, and `Switching Margin M`: live distance tuning. Defaults are 125, 350, 750 and 25 m.
- `Chunk Size M`: defaults to 250 m. Set before starting the scene; cells are generated once at startup.
- Controller `Visible`: hides replacement geometry while enabled. It does not disable actual light pools. Hide the parent `NightLights` to hide fixtures and illumination together.

No offline rebake is required: cells use the current generated fixture layout and spacing at startup. A standalone lighting node creates the same controller if an authored child isn't supplied.

## Changed files

- `scripts/city_night_lights.gd`: moves original batches under OriginalFixtures, initializes chunk geometry, forwards night changes, and selects pavement lights with 3D distance.
- `scripts/streetlamp_chunks.gd`: spatial cells, single-surface full/simple/head meshes, distance hysteresis, cutoff, comparison controls and loading state.
- `assets/sky/streetlamp_chunks.gdshader`: shared metal/lens material with district color and night emission.
- `scenes/super_city.tscn`: adds the authored StreetlampChunks controller under NightLights.
- `tests/test_streetlamp_chunks.gd`: complete fixture coverage, actual MultiMesh transform/color readback, geometry budgets, thresholds, restoration, day/night behavior and high-flight lighting culling.

## Validation

Godot 4.7.2 tests passed: the new streetlamp suite on the D3D12 renderer, existing city night-light integration, and urban-night lighting tests. Headless checks also pass; the headless dummy renderer cannot read MultiMesh instance data, so placement/color readback assertions are explicitly limited to the graphical run. Full/detail/simple/head-only captures were rendered and inspected under `artifacts/streetlamp_chunks/`, including daylight and night. No FPS benchmark or manual traversal playtest was performed.

For a deterministic geometry-only check at camera `(0,3,0)` at night, the configured cells selected 17,616 triangles before camera culling, compared with the old 338,124 full-fixture total. At `(0,1000,0)`, all cells were beyond the cutoff. These are configuration counts, not rendered-frame measurements.

Restart Main. Walk beside streetlamps to check their shape and illumination, then fly down a long corridor and away from the city to watch the simplified/head-only/cutoff levels. Test both noon and midnight and fly above 500 m to check that pavement lighting no longer follows from far below. Compare using Enabled at a fixed view, and restore Enabled/Automatic afterward.
